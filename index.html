The opportunity

The gap is huge.
And it is getting wider.

People want to live longer and perform better. The science exists. The wearables exist. The data exists. What is missing is a trusted, accessible brand that translates all of it into clear, actionable guidance — without the pseudoscience, without the personal cult, without the US-only access.

01

The research is moving fast

Longevity science — senolytics, GLP-1s, NAD+, Zone 2, biological age testing — is crossing from academic journals into mainstream awareness. Most people have no idea what to do with it.

02

Wearables without guidance

Millions of people are wearing Oura, Whoop, and CGMs. They have more health data than ever. Almost none of them know how to act on it.

03

EU is underserved

The best consumer healthtech companies — Levels, Function Health, Superpower — are US-only. European consumers want privacy-first, GDPR-native health tools. Nobody is building them.

04

AI makes it possible

Personalised health guidance previously cost €500/hour from a nutritionist or performance coach. AI makes it possible to deliver that quality of guidance at scale, to everyone.
