This Privacy Policy describes how Proco Technologies Limited (Irish company No. 670796, "Proco," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal data when you use our website and services at procohq.com (the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and processing your data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018.

2. What Data We Collect

a. Data you provide directly

  • Email address — when you join our waitlist or early access programme
  • Name — if provided during registration or account setup
  • Health and biomarker data — blood test results, biomarker values, and health metrics when you use our testing service (see Section 4)
  • Health history and lifestyle information — if voluntarily provided to personalise your longevity plan
  • Communications — messages you send us via email or contact forms

b. Data collected automatically

  • Usage analytics — pages visited, time on site, and interaction patterns via PostHog (EU-hosted)
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Cookies — see Section 8 below

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Manage your waitlist registration and send you early access updates
  • Provide and personalise the biomarker testing and longevity planning service
  • Generate and deliver your personalised health reports and recommendations
  • Send transactional communications (order confirmations, test results, account alerts)
  • Send marketing emails about Proco — only with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Improve the Service and develop new features (using aggregated, anonymised data)
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support
  • Comply with our legal obligations

4. Health Data — Special Category Processing

Biomarker and health information is special category personal data under Article 9 of the GDPR. We apply the highest level of protection to this data.

Lawful basis: We process your health data only on the basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). You will be asked to give explicit consent before any health data is collected or processed.

What this means for you:

  • You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting privacy@procohq.com
  • Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal
  • If you withdraw consent, we will stop processing your health data and delete it unless we are required to retain it by law

Health data is processed by accredited third-party laboratory partners for the purpose of conducting blood analysis. These partners act as data processors under our instruction and are bound by data processing agreements that meet GDPR requirements.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Waitlist and early access communications: Legitimate interests (managing our pre-launch programme) and/or consent
  • Marketing emails: Consent — you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Health/biomarker data: Explicit consent
  • Service delivery: Performance of a contract
  • Legal compliance: Legal obligation

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Laboratory partners — accredited third-party labs that conduct blood analysis, acting under our instruction as data processors
  • Service providers — hosting, analytics (PostHog), email delivery, and other vendors who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements
  • Legal obligations — if required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality obligations and notice to you

Any third-party processors are required to process your data only on our instructions, with appropriate security measures in place.

7. Data Retention

  • Waitlist email addresses: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion, or until 2 years after the waitlist closes if you have not become a customer
  • Account data: Retained while your account is active, plus 2 years after account closure for legal and dispute resolution purposes
  • Health and biomarker data: Retained for 5 years from the date of collection, or until you request deletion (subject to any legal retention requirements)
  • Analytics data: Retained for 12 months in anonymised or aggregated form

You may request earlier deletion at any time — see Section 9.

8. Cookies

We use the following cookies on procohq.com:

  • Analytics cookies (PostHog, EU-hosted) — to understand how visitors use the site. You can opt out via our cookie banner or at any time by contacting us.
  • Essential cookies — necessary for the site to function (e.g. session management). These cannot be disabled.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels for third-party ad networks.

9. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time for consent-based processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@procohq.com. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for exercising your rights.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) at dataprotection.ie.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure — if you become aware of any security concerns, please contact us at hello@procohq.com.

11. International Transfers

We endeavour to store and process your data within the European Economic Area (EEA). Where data is transferred outside the EEA (for example, to certain service providers), we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

12. Children

The Service is not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a minor, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email (if we hold your email address) or by posting a notice on our website. The updated policy will be effective from the "Last updated" date above.
